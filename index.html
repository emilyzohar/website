About

I am a PhD student in Experimental Social Psychology at the University of Toronto. My research focuses on the psychology of effort: how people perceive effort, when they avoid it, when they embrace it, and how social and technological contexts shape their willingness to do hard things.

My work examines effort norms, effort normalization, mindsets about the negative feelings of effort, and the cues people use to judge effort in others. I also study how emerging technologies, including AI, may change how willing people are to think, persist, and work, as well as how everyday environments such as offices shape anticipated and objective productivity.
